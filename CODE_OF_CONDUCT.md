# Code of conduct

Den follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: we want Den's community to be welcoming and harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Be kind and assume good intent. Give and accept constructive feedback
gracefully, focus on what is best for the community, and take responsibility
for your mistakes. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks,
sexualized language or imagery, and publishing others' private information
are not acceptable.

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and
chat) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers. **Before the
repository goes public, the maintainers will publish a reporting address
here.** Every report will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly,
and the privacy of the reporter will be respected. Maintainers who do not
follow the code of conduct may face consequences determined by other members
of the project's leadership.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
